# Migi

**Japanese that finds you.** Everyday Japanese on your Home Screen, Lock Screen and Apple
Watch, with an occasional one-tap check to see what's sticking.

- **No study sessions.** Glance at your widget a few times a day. That's it.
- **Japanese for your life.** Food, plans, friends, travel: you choose what matters.
- **Casual and polite, in context.** Learn what to say to a friend and what to say to a shop
  assistant.
- **No streaks. No nagging.** Notifications are off unless you want them.
- **Private by design.** No account, no ads, no tracking.

Free to use, with an optional Migi Plus for more topics and unlimited practice.
