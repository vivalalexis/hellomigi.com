# Migi Privacy Policy

Effective {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}}

Migi helps you pick up everyday Japanese on your iPhone and Apple Watch. It works without
an account, and it's designed to know as little about you as possible. This policy explains what
the app does with information, and your choices.

## Who we are

Migi is made by {{OWNER_LEGAL_NAME}}, an individual developer based in Australia ("we",
"us"). For anything about privacy, email **tk@hellomigi.com**.

## The short version

- No accounts, no ads, no tracking across apps or websites, and we never sell or share your
  information.
- Your learning progress stays on your devices and in **your own iCloud**. We can't see it.
- The app can send **anonymous usage statistics** so we can tell whether it's helping people
  learn. You can switch this off at any time in Settings. In the EU and EEA it's off unless
  you switch it on.

## What the app stores on your devices

Your learning history (which Japanese items were shown, your quiz answers, your settings and
the "My Life" topics you choose) is stored on your iPhone and Apple Watch so the app can decide
what to show you next. It never leaves your devices except through iCloud sync (below), and
it's deleted when you delete the app or use **Settings → Delete my data**.

## iCloud sync

If you're signed in to iCloud, the app saves your learning history to **your private iCloud
database** so it can sync between your devices and be restored on a new phone. This data is
stored by Apple under [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). We can't
access your private iCloud database. You can turn this off by turning off iCloud for the app in
iOS Settings, and **Delete my data** removes it from iCloud too.

## Anonymous usage statistics

**What's sent:** short, structured signals about how the app is used, for example "a
recognition quiz on item *word.oishii* was answered correctly 1 to 3 days after it was last
shown, on the home-screen widget". Signals include item identifiers from our content, quiz
results, which part of the app was used, rough time ranges, and whether you have Plus. Our
analytics provider adds basic technical details: OS version, device model, language and region
settings, accessibility settings (such as text size), and the hour and day of the week.

**What's never sent:** your name, email, Apple Account, contacts, location, the text of any
problem report, your "My Life" topics, or anything you type.

**How it's kept anonymous:** we use [TelemetryDeck](https://telemetrydeck.com), based in Germany.
It works out a random per-install code from your device's app-vendor identifier, scrambles it
(hashes it with a secret value) on your device, and scrambles it again on its servers. This is
designed so that neither we nor TelemetryDeck can turn that code back into your device or identity. We use it only to
count things like "how many people used a widget this week".

**Why:** to understand whether the app is helping people learn and to improve it, for
example which quiz types work and whether people keep using the widgets.

**Your choice:**

- **EU and EEA:** statistics are **off** unless you choose "Share anonymous stats" during setup
  or in Settings (consent).
- **Everywhere else:** statistics are **on** by default, and you can switch them off at any time
  in setup or **Settings → Share anonymous stats**. When it's off, the analytics component isn't
  started at all and nothing is sent.

**How long:** TelemetryDeck keeps the statistics for as long as our plan allows, and never more
than 25 months.

## Purchases

Purchases of Migi Plus are handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment
details. The app checks with Apple, on your device, whether you have Plus. Apple gives us sales
reports that don't identify you.

## Crash reports

If you've chosen to share analytics with app developers in iOS (Settings → Privacy & Security →
Analytics & Improvements), Apple may share anonymous crash reports with us. We use them only
to fix bugs.

## Who receives information

| Who | What | Why |
|---|---|---|
| TelemetryDeck GmbH (Germany, EU hosting) | Anonymous usage statistics, if they're on | Analytics, under a data processing agreement with us |
| Apple | Your iCloud data (stored in your account); purchases | Sync and payments, under Apple's own privacy policy |

We don't share information with anyone else, and we don't sell or "share" personal information
as those words are used in US state privacy laws.

## Legal bases

- **Usage statistics:** your **consent** if you're in the EU or EEA; elsewhere, our
  **legitimate interest** in understanding and improving the app, and you can object at any
  time by switching statistics off.
- **iCloud sync and Plus:** to provide the service you asked for.

## Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, object
to processing, or withdraw consent, by emailing tk@hellomigi.com. Most of this you can do
yourself in the app: switch statistics off, export your learning history, or delete your data.
Because usage statistics are anonymous, we usually can't find or delete the statistics that
came from a particular person; switching them off stops anything new being sent.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, please contact us first. You can
also complain to your privacy regulator: in Australia the
[Office of the Australian Information Commissioner](https://www.oaic.gov.au), or elsewhere your
local privacy or data protection authority.

## International transfers

Usage statistics are processed in the EU by TelemetryDeck. iCloud data is stored by Apple, which
may store it in other countries under its own safeguards.

## Children

Migi is made for adults and teenagers learning Japanese. It isn't directed at children
under 16, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top and describe important changes in the
app's release notes.
