# Migi Privacy Policy

Effective 6 October 2026

Migi helps you pick up everyday Japanese on your iPhone and Apple Watch. To keep your progress
on any device, Migi asks you to sign in. This policy explains what we collect, why, who helps us
handle it, and the choices you have.

## Who we are

Migi is made by Trisha Kuek, an individual developer based in Australia ("we", "us"). For
anything about privacy, email **tk@hellomigi.com**.

## The short version

- You sign in with Apple, Google or your email address. There are no passwords.
- Your account and your learning progress are stored in our database in **Sydney, Australia**,
  so they sync between your devices.
- The app sends **usage statistics linked to your account** so we can tell whether it's helping
  people learn. You can switch them off at any time. In the EU and EEA they're off unless you
  switch them on.
- No ads, no tracking across other companies' apps or websites, and we never sell your
  information.
- You can delete your account and everything in it from the app at any time.

## What we collect

**Your account**

- **Your email address.** If you sign in with Apple and choose "Hide My Email", this is a
  private relay address from Apple, and we never see your real one.
- **How you sign in** (Apple, Google or email), and an account ID our database creates for you.
- **If you sign in with Google:** Google also gives us your **name** and a link to your
  **profile photo**. We keep them with your account. We may use them in future to personalise
  your profile in the app and the messages we send you.
- **Your plan** (Free or Plus) and when it renews or ends.
- **Whether you've asked for news and tips by email**, and when you changed that choice.

**Your learning progress**

Which Japanese items the app has shown you, your quiz answers, your settings and the "My Life"
topics you choose. This history decides what the app shows you next. It's stored on your
devices and in your account, so it syncs between your devices and comes back when you sign in
on a new one.

**Usage statistics** (see below), if they're switched on.

We don't collect your contacts, your location, photos from your device, or anything you type
into the app.

## How we use it

- **To run Migi:** sign you in, keep your progress in sync, and know which plan you're on.
- **To send emails you need:** your sign-in code.
- **To send news and tips:** only if you tick "Send me occasional news and tips". Every such
  email has an unsubscribe link, and you can untick the box in Settings at any time.
- **To improve Migi:** through usage statistics, if they're on.

## Usage statistics

**What's sent:** short, structured signals about how the app is used, for example "a
recognition quiz on item *word.oishii* was answered correctly 1 to 3 days after it was last
shown, on the home-screen widget". Signals include item identifiers from our content, quiz
results, which part of the app was used, rough time ranges, and whether you have Plus. Our
analytics provider adds basic technical details: OS version, device model, language and region
settings, accessibility settings (such as text size), and the hour and day of the week.

**What's never sent:** your email, your name, the text of any problem report, your "My Life"
topics, or anything you type.

**How it's linked to you:** the statistics are tied to your account ID after it has been
scrambled (hashed) with a secret value on your device. Our analytics provider,
[TelemetryDeck](https://telemetrydeck.com), scrambles it again on its servers. Nothing is sent
before you sign in or after you sign out.

**Your choice:**

- **EU and EEA:** statistics are **off** unless you choose "Share usage stats" during setup or
  in Settings (consent).
- **Everywhere else:** statistics are **on** by default, and you can switch them off at any time
  in **Settings → Share usage stats**. When they're off, the analytics component isn't started
  and nothing is sent.

**How long:** TelemetryDeck keeps the statistics for as long as our plan allows, and never more
than 25 months.

## Purchases

Purchases of Migi Plus are handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details. When
you buy Plus, Apple tells our server which account it belongs to and when it renews or ends, so
your plan follows your account to any device. Apple gives us sales reports that don't identify
you.

## Crash reports

If you've chosen to share analytics with app developers in iOS (Settings → Privacy & Security →
Analytics & Improvements), Apple may share anonymous crash reports with us. We use them only to
fix bugs.

## Who helps us

| Who | What | Where | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Supabase | Your account, learning progress and plan | Sydney, Australia | Our database and sign-in service |
| Resend | Your email address and the emails we send you | Tokyo, Japan (Resend is a US company) | Sending sign-in codes, and news and tips if you asked for them |
| TelemetryDeck GmbH | Usage statistics, if they're on | Germany (EU) | Analytics |
| Apple | Sign in with Apple; purchases | Apple's own systems | Under Apple's privacy policy |
| Google | Sign in with Google | Google's own systems | Under Google's privacy policy |

Each works for us under its own data processing terms and may use the information only to
provide its service to us. We don't share your information with anyone else, and we don't sell
or "share" personal information as those words are used in US state privacy laws.

## International transfers

Your account and progress stay in Australia. Your email address is processed in Japan, and by a
US company, when we send you email. Usage statistics are processed in the EU. Apple and Google
handle their sign-in services under their own safeguards.

## How long we keep it

- **Your account and progress:** until you delete your account. Deleting it removes them from
  our database straight away. Copies may remain in our database backups for up to 30 days.
- **Signing out** erases the copy on that device. Your account keeps everything until you sign
  in again.
- **Usage statistics:** see above.

## Deleting your account

In the app, go to **Settings → Delete account**. This permanently deletes your account and your
learning progress on all devices, and removes you from our email list. If you signed in with
Apple, Apple asks you to confirm, and we cancel Migi's access to your Apple sign-in. Deleting
your account doesn't cancel an App Store subscription; you can do that in the App Store.

## Legal bases

- **Your account, progress and plan:** to provide the service you asked for.
- **News and tips emails:** your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.
- **Usage statistics:** your **consent** if you're in the EU or EEA; elsewhere, our
  **legitimate interest** in understanding and improving the app, and you can object at any time
  by switching statistics off.

## Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us what we hold about you, ask for a copy, ask us to correct or
delete it, object to processing, or withdraw consent, by emailing tk@hellomigi.com. You can do
much of this yourself in the app: switch statistics off, untick news and tips, or delete your
account.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, please contact us first. You can
also complain to your privacy regulator: in Australia the
[Office of the Australian Information Commissioner](https://www.oaic.gov.au), or elsewhere your
local privacy or data protection authority.

## Security

Sign-in uses Apple, Google or a one-time email code, so there are no passwords to steal. Your
data travels over encrypted connections, and our database lets each account read and change only
its own records. The keys that unlock everyone's data stay on our server and are never in the
app.

## Children

Migi is made for adults and teenagers learning Japanese. It isn't directed at children under 16,
and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top and describe important changes in the
app's release notes.
