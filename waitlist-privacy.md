# Migi Launch List Privacy Notice

Effective 19 October 2026

Before Migi is on the App Store, hellomigi.com has a form where you can ask to be emailed when it
launches. This notice covers only that list. The Migi app has its own
[privacy policy](/privacy/).

## Who we are

Migi is made by Trisha Kuek, an individual developer based in Australia ("we", "us"). For
anything about privacy, email **tk@hellomigi.com**.

## Separate from Migi accounts

Joining the list doesn't create a Migi account, signing in to the app doesn't add you to it, and
we don't link the two, even if you use the same email address for both.

## What we collect

- Your email address.
- Whether you ticked "Also send me occasional updates".
- If you reached the form through a link from one of our social accounts, a short tag naming it
  (for example "threads" or "instagram"), so we can count where people find us. Coming to
  hellomigi.com directly stores no tag.
- Whether you've unsubscribed, and the date you signed up.

We don't ask for your name or anything else.

**Signing up is your consent:** when you submit the form, you're on the list straight away. We
don't send a confirmation email. We keep the date you signed up, because Australian law asks
senders to be able to show that people agreed to their emails.

**Stopping misuse:** to stop the form being flooded, we allow only a few sign-ups an hour from each
internet connection. For this we keep a scrambled version of the IP address of each sign-up (a
salted hash), never the IP address itself. These records are deleted once
they're a day old.

## What you'll get

- **One email when Migi launches**, with the App Store link.
- **Occasional updates** about Migi, a few a year at most, only if you ticked the box.

We don't use your address for anything else, and we never sell it or give it to advertisers.

## Who handles it

| Who | What | Where | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Supabase | The list, in its own table, not linked to any account | Sydney, Australia | Our database and sign-up service |
| Resend | Your email address and the emails we send you | Tokyo, Japan (Resend is a US company) | Sending the launch and update emails; keeping addresses on our launch list until they leave it |

Each works for us under its own data processing terms and may use the information only to
provide its service to us.

**On our website:** hellomigi.com has no analytics or tracking scripts, and the form doesn't add
any. It sends what you enter only to our own sign-up service on Supabase.

## Unsubscribing

Every email we send you has a one-click unsubscribe link. It takes you off
the list straight away and removes your address from Resend, unless you also get emails from
the Migi app. You can also email
tk@hellomigi.com, and we'll take you off within 5 working days.

## How long we keep it

- If you asked only for the launch email, we delete your address within 30 days after the launch
  email is sent.
- If you asked for updates, we keep it until you unsubscribe, then delete it within 30 days.

## Legal basis and your rights

We rely on your **consent** (submitting the form), which you can withdraw at any
time by unsubscribing. You can also ask us what we hold about you, ask for a copy, or ask us to
correct or delete it, by emailing tk@hellomigi.com.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, please contact us first. You can also
complain to your privacy regulator: in Australia the
[Office of the Australian Information Commissioner](https://www.oaic.gov.au), or elsewhere your
local privacy or data protection authority.

## Age

The launch list is for people 16 and over.

## Changes

If this notice changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page and tell the people on the
list by email.
